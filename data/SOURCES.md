# Data Sources

## Text corpora (punctuation model training)
- NLTK `state_union` corpus – US State of the Union addresses (public domain)
- NLTK `inaugural` corpus  – US Inaugural Addresses (public domain)

These are real speech transcripts used as text only.
They are NOT audio; they are NOT real Whisper output.
